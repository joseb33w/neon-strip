# PLAN — Neon Strip (open-world neon-desert city, Las Vegas style)

A chunk-streamed Godot 4.6.3 mobile-web game built on the RPG streaming template
(`mode:"chunk"`), extended with bespoke driving, interiors, casino games, maps, and
Supabase persistence. Style: stylized low-poly neon-desert (kaykit `kk_city` kit +
Kenney pedestrians + Quaternius/Kenney flora + Meshy hero pieces + neon emission).

## Goal
Walk OR drive a believable ~4x4-chunk neon-desert Strip: tall casino/hotel towers with
neon, side blocks, drivable highways, sandy desert edges. Talk to people, collect
cash/chips/items, play casino games, take stairs/elevator up a venue, check a map, watch
day -> night cycle, with localized sound and working Supabase save.

## Out of scope
Multiplayer; full traffic simulation/AI pathing; persistent NPC schedules; real-money anything;
more than ~2 floors per venue; saving exact car position.
